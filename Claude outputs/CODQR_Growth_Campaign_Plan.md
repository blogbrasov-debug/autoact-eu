## 💰 **PHASE 3: Monetization Tests (Days 15-60)**

### Test 1: Contextual Upsells (Days 15-45)
**Trigger-based messaging** (implement in app):
- User creates 5+ codes → Banner: "See who scans your codes? Go Business for 29 lei/month"
- User exports 3+ codes → Email: "Track your QR performance with Analytics"
- **Expected lift**: +10-15% free users → paid

**Implementation**: 
- Add conditional JavaScript in app.js (check code count)
- Show contextual modal/banner
- A/B test messaging (test 2 versions)

### Test 2: Vertical-Specific Bundles (Days 20-50)
Create 3 pricing tiers targeting SMBs:

**Bundle A: "Restaurant QR Starter Pack" (49 lei/month)**
- 50 QR codes/month
- 5 pre-designed menu templates
- Basic analytics (scan count)
- 3-month lock-in (commitment signals quality)
- Target: Solo restaurants, food courts

**Bundle B: "Retail Inventory Kit" (39 lei/month)**
- 100 QR codes/month
- CSV import/batch export
- Asset tracking template
- Target: Small shops, warehouses

**Bundle C: "Event Organizer Pro" (59 lei/month)**
- 200 QR codes/month
- Ticketing dashboard
- Attendee analytics
- Target: Event planners, venues

**How to sell**:
- Show on pricing page (new section: "Pre-built for...")
- Email existing free users (monthly digest: "Your industry is using X")
- Blog CTA: After reading "Restaurant QR Guide" → "See the Restaurant Bundle"

### Test 3: Pro Pricing Optimization (Days 30-60)
A/B test: 19 lei vs 24 lei vs 29 lei per code
- **Track**: Conversion rate, revenue per transaction
- **Hypothesis**: Higher price signals quality to some segments
- **Plan**: Run 2-week tests, measure impact

### Test 4: Freemium Cap (Conservative, Days 45-90)
**Current**: Unlimited free codes
**Test**: Free = 10 codes/month, then paywall

**Risk**: Lower MAU | **Benefit**: Forces micro-conversions
**Approach**: 
- Subtle: No hard paywall, just "Unlock unlimited?" at 11th code
- Watch metrics before implementing
